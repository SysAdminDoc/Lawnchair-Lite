Lawnchair Lite gives you a calm, flexible Android home screen without adding ads, analytics, or another account.

Shape the launcher around your phone:
• Multiple home pages with folders, widgets, an adjustable dock, and six page transitions
• Six dark themes, Material You color, custom accents, icon packs, shapes, and font import
• App drawer tabs for All, Recent, Favorites, and Work
• Tablet and foldable layouts that keep the workspace visible beside the drawer

Find what you need:
• Fast fuzzy matching for app names and imperfect typing
• Local intent phrases such as “my gym app”
• Calculator and unit conversion results inside search
• Optional local contact results and user-triggered web search

Keep control of your data:
• No ads or sponsored placements
• No analytics or behavior tracking
• No cloud account required
• No Firebase or proprietary crash reporting
• Contacts, calendar events, alarms, and notification badges are processed locally
• Manual backups let you choose which settings and private fields to include
• Restore preview checks an import before applying it

Optional weather uses Open-Meteo after coarse location access is granted. Android Auto Backup excludes launcher preferences.
